Our story

What started in our kitchen became a journey of healing.

Lely's Bread began with a simple desire: to nourish our family with food that did more than fill us up.

A different way to think about flour.

About a year into our journey, we began learning more about fresh-milled flour and the incredible difference it can make. We discovered that when wheat is freshly milled, it keeps more of the vitamins, minerals, healthy fats, and natural character of the whole grain.

The more we learned, the more we realized that food was meant to nourish us, not simply fill us up.

As we started baking with fresh-milled flour, we experienced firsthand how much thoughtful ingredients can change the way food tastes and feels. What began as a choice for our own family quickly became a passion to share that same nourishment with others.

Made with love.

Our mission is not just to bake bread. It is to help bring real food back to the table and serve our community one loaf at a time.
