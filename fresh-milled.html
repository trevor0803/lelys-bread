Why fresh-milled?

Flour is at its best when it is fresh.

We mill whole grains close to bake time to preserve more of their natural flavor, aroma, texture, and character.

From whole grain to finished bake, every step is handled with intention.

The difference

Whole grain goodness, used closer to the moment.

Commercial flour is often processed and stored long before it reaches a kitchen. Fresh milling keeps the grain's bran, germ, and endosperm together and turns it into flour shortly before baking.
