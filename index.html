Why fresh-milled flour?

Because flour is at its best when it is fresh.

Fresh milling keeps more of the whole grain together and brings a deeper, naturally satisfying flavor to every loaf, roll, and treat.

01

Whole-grain goodness

The bran, germ, and endosperm remain part of the flour rather than being separated during refining.

02

Naturally rich flavor

Fresh flour has warmth, aroma, and character that long-stored flour simply cannot imitate.

03

More satisfying texture

Whole-grain fiber adds body and helps create baked goods that feel substantial, not empty.

04

Made close to the moment

We mill with baking in mind, then turn that flour into something worth sharing.

Before you order.

How do I place an order?

Tap “Request an order” or choose a menu item. Your phone's messaging app will open with a prefilled message. Send it to ask about availability and arrange the order.

Is this an online checkout?

No. This site is designed for personal order requests by text. Lely's Bread will confirm availability, timing, pickup, and payment details directly.

Does the menu change?

Yes. Lely's Bread bakes in small batches, so flavors and items can change. The menu section pulls current public product information from Hotplate whenever available.

What makes fresh-milled flour different?

It keeps the components of the whole grain together and is used close to milling, creating a fuller whole-grain flavor and a more substantial texture.
