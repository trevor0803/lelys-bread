Our story

It began with a desire to nourish our family.

As we learned about fresh-milled flour and began baking with more intentional ingredients, we experienced the difference for ourselves. What started in our own kitchen became a passion to share that same care with others.

Today, every Lely's Bread bake is made to help bring real food back to the table and serve our community one loaf at a time.

Good to know

Before you order.

How do I place an order?

Tap any Order Now button to open Lely's Bread on Hotplate. Choose the items and quantities you want, then complete your order there.

Does the menu change?

Yes. Lely's Bread works in small batches, so items and flavors may change from one bread drop to the next.

Why do products only appear once here?

Different quantities or package sizes are grouped under one product on this website. You can choose the available option when you open Hotplate.

What makes fresh-milled flour different?

Fresh milling keeps the parts of the whole grain together and uses the flour close to milling, creating fuller flavor and a more substantial whole-grain texture.
